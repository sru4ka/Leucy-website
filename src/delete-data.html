Delete your data

Effective {{effective}}

You are in control of your Leucy data. Here is how to remove it, depending on where it is stored.

1. Data on your iPhone

  1. Open Leucy and tap Profile.
  2. Open Privacy and data, and tap Export my data first if you want a copy.
  3. Tap Delete all my data and confirm.

This erases every meal, dose, side effect, weight, photo and setting stored by the App on that device, cancels its reminders and signs you out. Deleting the App also removes its data from the phone. Copies may remain in your own iCloud or computer backups until those backups are replaced.

2. Your account and cloud backup

This applies only if you created an account (with Apple, Google or an email and password).

In the App

  1. Open Leucy and tap Profile.
  2. Open Account and backup and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. If you signed in with Apple, confirm with Apple when asked.
  4. Choose whether to keep the data on this iPhone or erase it too.

Your account and every backed-up meal, dose, side effect, weight and setting are deleted from our database straight away. Copies in our hosting provider's encrypted backups are overwritten within 7 days. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription, because Apple bills it: cancel it in iPhone Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions.

Without the App

  1. Email {{privacy_email}} with the subject "Delete my Leucy account".
  2. Send it from the email address on your account. If you used Sign in with Apple with "Hide My Email", send it from the Apple ID email that forwards to that address, or include the private relay address.
  3. We confirm your request and delete your account and every backed-up record within 30 days.

You can also stop Leucy from using your sign-in at any time. Apple: on your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Sign-In and Security, then Sign in with Apple, choose Leucy and tap Stop Using Apple ID. Google: open your Google Account, then Security, then Your connections to third-party apps and services, choose Leucy and remove its access.

3. Other data

  • Meal photos and coach messages are not stored on our servers, so there is nothing to delete.
  • Analytics events contain no health data and are linked only to a random identifier. Deleting all data in the App resets that identifier. Events are kept for up to 24 months; email us if you want those linked to your device removed sooner.
  • Purchase records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat as required for tax and accounting.
  • Support emails can be deleted on request.
