Why protein first

Lose the fat. Keep the muscle.

When appetite drops, meals get small and protein is often the first thing to go. Getting enough protein and staying active are widely recommended ways to help protect lean muscle while you lose weight.

  • A daily protein target built from your body and your goal, which you can change any time.
  • A 2 pm nudge if you are behind, with low-volume foods that fit a small appetite.
  • Maintenance mode for when you taper or stop, so protein stays first as your plan changes.

Your provider can tell you the right protein target for you.

Questions

Good to know

Does Leucy tell me what dose to take?

No. Leucy never recommends, changes or questions a dose. It records the doses you enter and shows them back to you. Questions about dosing, missed doses or switching medication belong with your prescriber.

How accurate is the photo scan?

It is an estimate. A vision model identifies the foods and portion sizes and shows a confidence score. You can adjust the portion, edit any item, or use a barcode or text entry instead. For packaged foods, a barcode is usually the most accurate.

Is the medication-level curve my real blood level?

No. It is an educational estimate based on published half-lives and the doses you log, shown in relative units. It is not a measurement and should never guide dosing.

Do I need an account?

No. Leucy works without an account. If you want backup across devices, you can opt in to Sign in with Apple and cloud sync from Profile.

Which devices are supported?

Leucy is built for iPhone. Apple Health sync and home and lock screen widgets are included.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Leucy, and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.
