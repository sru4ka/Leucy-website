Medical Disclaimer

Effective November 9, 2026

If you think you are having a medical emergency, call your local emergency number now. Contact your healthcare provider straight away if you have severe or persistent stomach pain, cannot keep fluids down, have signs of dehydration, an allergic reaction, a lump or swelling in your neck, or any symptom that worries you.

Leucy is for tracking and education

Leucy helps you record and understand your own information: meals, protein, doses you have been prescribed, side effects and weight. It is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease, and it has not been evaluated or cleared by the US Food and Drug Administration or any other regulator.

We never recommend a dose

The App does not tell you what dose to take, when to increase or decrease it, what to do about a missed or late dose, or whether to switch medication. Dose amounts and escalation plans in the App are only what you enter. The food coach is built to refuse these questions and point you to your prescriber. Always follow your healthcare provider and the instructions that come with your medication.

Estimates are estimates

  • Medication-level curve. This is an educational model based on published half-lives and the doses you log. It is shown in relative units and is not a measurement of the medication in your body. Real levels depend on many personal factors.
  • Nutrition. Calories, protein and other values from photos, menus, text and barcodes are estimates and can be wrong.
  • Targets. Protein, calorie, water and fiber targets are general starting points. Your provider or a registered dietitian may recommend different ones, especially if you have kidney disease, diabetes, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or have other conditions.
  • Insights. Patterns between doses and side effects describe what you logged. They are not a diagnosis and do not mean one caused the other.
  • Weight projections and milestones. These are illustrations, not predictions or promises of results.

Talk to your provider

Do not start, stop or change any medication, diet or exercise plan based on the App alone. Never disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking it because of something you read in the App or on this Site. You can share the App's PDF report with your provider to support that conversation.
